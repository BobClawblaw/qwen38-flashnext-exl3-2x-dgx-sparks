# Changelog

This recipe's own changes, newest first. It descends from the MLX recipe (BobClawblaw/qwen38-flashnext-tensorfold-2x-dgx-sparks) and, through it, from sfxnz's base recipe; their histories are in their repositories.

## 2026-10-07

- **A start waits out the settle after a stop.** `stop.sh` stamps `.run-state/stopped_at`; `run.sh settle_after_stop` sleeps the remainder of `SETTLE_SECONDS` (30) before rank 1 starts. `systemctl restart` runs stop.sh and then run.sh within seconds, and the worker refused NCCL's first memory registration (`ibv_reg_mr_iova2: Cannot allocate memory`) in that window on the MLX pair on 2026-10-07; the earlier settle only ran when a container was still there to stop. The MLX recipe now ships this patch as its default (`flashnext-0.6.6.patch`, same bytes, same pin) with its own `PROFILE=long`.
- **First release: turboderp's EXL3 pack (4.05 bpw) of Qwen3.8-Flash-Next on two DGX Sparks at TP=2.** TensorFold 0.6.6 served EXL3 packs on one GPU only; the patch's part 6 splits the pack across two ranks on whole 16x16 tiles and whole 128-wide Hadamard blocks (q with its gates, k, v, the DeltaNet projections and the head by outputs; o_proj and out_proj by inputs with gathered partials) and shares the 640-wide experts whole, 256 a rank plus the shared expert in turn, with the picks remapped and the skipped slots' weights zeroed. Found and fixed on the way: the engine returned a non-MLX output projection as a finished branch on two ranks (each rank kept half the heads), and the fused down-and-combine kernel reads a skipped slot's stale output from the shared scratch. 41.4 GiB a rank; the full 262,144 window on both ranks.
- **1M context (`PROFILE=long`).** Part 7 reads Qwen's YaRN block from `config.json` (factor 4 over the 262,144 trained window, as the model card prescribes), computes the frequencies as transformers does and folds the attention factor into the rotated norm scales. `run.sh` builds a profile folder of links plus its own `config.json`; the pack is untouched. 58 GiB a rank at the 1M window on int8 KV.
- **Sixteen users on an EXL3 pack.** Part 8 lifts a 128-row cap in the EXL3 path's fp16 hyper-connection projection that stopped any EXL3 serve above eight streams, on one GPU or two.
- **Images and video, tools, copy drafts** come from the MLX recipe's patch (parts 1 to 5, byte for byte). The pack's EXL3 vision tower is converted to fp16 once by `run.sh` (`VISION=1`); the vision probe answers with the same reply hash as the MLX pair.
- **Quality gates on the shipped image** (`evidence/s1-exl3-tp2/quality/`): GSM8K 239/250, IFEval 103/120, tools 60/60, repeated 4-grams 64/64, `reasoning_effort` 5/5 on the concurrent profile; JSON schema 30/30 on the serial profile; `score.py t2` against the MLX pair's run PASS (2 lost / 2 gained on GSM8K, 6 / 7 on IFEval).
- **Measured** (`evidence/s1-exl3-tp2/`): serial profile one user prose 57 / structured 239 tok/s; concurrent profile with vision 54 / 234 at one user, 161 and 264 aggregate at 4 and 8 users (prose), 369 prose and 798 structured at 16; drafted replies equal undrafted on every prompt; two ranks equal one GPU to the last byte on 4 of 6 greedy replies and diverge at a near-tie on the other two; the one-GPU reference on this pack decodes 54 prose / 81 code drafted.
